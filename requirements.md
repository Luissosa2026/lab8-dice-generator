# Lab 8: Dice Generator
Devs Inc

- Choose number of dice faces.
- Can roll to see outcome.

## User Stories

1. As a player, I want to choose any 2 dice from D2–D20.
2. Once the choice is made, I want to click the "Roll" button to get a random result.
3. I want to know the outcome of each die and the total.
4. I want a button that says "Start Over" to pick my dice combo again.

## Storyboard

1. Choose 2 dice.
2. Pick the faces for dice #1 and #2, such as D7 and D11.
3. Click Roll.
4. See the results, such as 1 and 9, with a total of 10.
5. Click Start Over to return to choosing 2 dice.

## Optional Extra
- Casino noise when rolling.
